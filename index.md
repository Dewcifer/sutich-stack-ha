# Sutich Stack HA

Sutich Stack HA is a private, single-user smart home system built on
Home Assistant (open-source home automation software) and run by its
owner, Dewey, on his own server in Lander, Wyoming.

## What the app does
It connects to the owner's own Google account so that his Home Assistant
installation can:
- read his Google Calendar events, so the house can announce his
  upcoming schedule and adjust routines around it, and
- read and update his Google Tasks, so his grocery and to-do lists stay
  in sync between his phone, his watch and his smart home.

## Who uses it
Only the owner. The app is not a public service, has no other users,
and does not offer sign-ups.
